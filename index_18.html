Peter Hammer

Documentary filmmaker & creative producer working in cinema and broadcast. I'm based in Copenhagen (and sometimes Amsterdam) and currently also teaching at the National Film School here. I hold an M.A in artistic research through cinema from Master of Film at Netherlands Film Academy, and a Bachelor of Journalism from DMJX.

A new site is under way. I'm available for consultancy and advisory work — please get in touch.
